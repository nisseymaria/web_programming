About our resort

Escape to a world where lush greenery meets the tranquil rhythm of Kerala’s backwaters. Situated directly on the scenic banks of Vembanad Lake, Paradise Resort offers the perfect blend of traditional heritage architecture and modern hospitality. Wake up to the soothing sounds of nature, watch the sun set over the infinite horizon, and experience the iconic charm of "God's Own Country".
