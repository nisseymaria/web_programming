My Favorite Place

Munnar is a world-famous hill station located at an altitude of 1,600 metres in the Idukki district of Kerala, India. Situated at the confluence of three mountain streams—Muthirapuzha, Nallathanni, and Kundala—its name literally translates to "three rivers" in Malayalam. Renowned for its sprawling tea plantations, misty valleys, and unique biodiversity, it remains one of South India's most popular holiday getaways,

The region has been inhabited by hunter-gatherer tribals like the Malayarayan and Muthuvan for thousands of years.[7] In the early days, only Tamils and few Malayalis lived there. They were brought as workers in the tea plantations. Tradition states that Colonel Arthur Wellesley, later the Duke of Wellington, was the first British person to pass through Munnar[8] during Tipu Sultan's campaign in Travancore, but this is unsubstantiated.

Munnar is a breathtaking hill station that captivates visitors with its endless rolling hills covered in vibrant green tea plantations. The refreshing mountain air and crisp climate offer an instant escape from the chaotic pace of everyday city life. Watching the dramatic morning mist slowly lift over the quiet, glassy waters of Mattupetty Dam creates an unforgettable sense of peace.

Munnar is beautiful, peaceful and refreshing.
